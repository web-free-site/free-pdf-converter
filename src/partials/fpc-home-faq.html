Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about privacy, cost, and how these tools run on your device.

Is Free PDF Converter really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no trial period, no watermark on exports, and no daily cap. You do not need an account or payment details.

Are my PDFs uploaded to your servers?

No. Files you select stay on your device. Conversion, merging, compression, and OCR run inside your browser. We do not receive a copy of your documents for processing.

Do I need to install software or sign up?

You only need a current version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. Open a tool, pick your file, and download the result. There is no desktop installer and no registration form.

Which devices are supported?

Any phone, tablet, or computer with a modern browser can run these tools. Large files may take longer on low-end hardware, but the privacy model is the same everywhere.

Is the software open source?

This site is based on open-source PDF tooling under the AGPL-3.0 license. The footer links to the upstream project and license text required by that license.

Can I use the tools offline?

After the first visit, many tools work offline because scripts and WebAssembly modules are cached. You still need to load each tool page at least once while online unless you host your own build.
