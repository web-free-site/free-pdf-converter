Why use this site?

Free PDF Converter is built for people who want desktop-grade results without handing files to a remote server.

Private by default

Processing uses memory inside your tab. Your PDFs are not sent to us for conversion, storage, or training.

In your browser

WebAssembly and modern PDF libraries run locally, so you get fast feedback without installing desktop software.

No upload step

You choose a file from your device and download the result when the job finishes. There is no cloud inbox in the middle.

No sign-up

Open a tool and start working. No email, password, or subscription is required to use the catalog.

How it works

The same three steps apply whether you merge two reports or export a scan to Word.

Choose a tool

Open a page for the job you need, or use the searchable grid below for the full list of converters and editors.

Select your file

Pick a PDF or image from your computer or phone. The file stays on your device while the tool loads the pages it needs.

Download the result

Adjust any options, run the task, and save the output. Close the tab when you are done; nothing is kept on our servers.
