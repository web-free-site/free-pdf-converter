Free PDF Converter is a privacy-first site where merge, split, compress, convert, and dozens of other PDF jobs run entirely in your browser. No account, no uploads, and no waiting on a remote server to “process” your documents.

The app is maintained by Gentil M. in Kigali, Rwanda. It is based on the open-source Free PDF Converter project (AGPL-3.0). We keep attribution, publish our changes, and thank the Free PDF Converter community for the engine underneath.

Revenue comes only from clearly labeled advertising. Core PDF tools stay free. If you need the source, use the Contact page or the “Source code” link in the footer.

Why Free PDF Converter?

Built for Speed

There's no upload or download step. Files are processed right in your browser using WebAssembly, so you're never waiting on a server.

Free to Use

Every tool is free, with no trials, no subscriptions and no watermarks on your PDFs. The site is supported by honest, labeled ads—not by selling your files or your data.

No Account Required

Open any tool and start right away. No email, no password, no personal information.

Open Source

This deployment’s source is available from the footer. The core derives from Free PDF Converter (AGPL-3.0) and libraries such as PDF.js, pdf-lib, qpdf, and LibreOffice WASM. You can verify that processing stays on your device.
