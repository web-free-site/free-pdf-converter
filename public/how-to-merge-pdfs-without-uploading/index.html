How to merge PDFs without uploading

Merging is useful when you have a scan, a form, and an extra appendix as separate files. Free PDF Converter can join them in the browser so you do not have to send the documents to a website to get one file back.

Steps

  1. Open the merge tool. From the Free PDF Converter home page, open the merge PDF tool.
  2. Add every file you want to join. Select the PDFs from your device. Add them in the order they should appear, or rearrange them if the tool lets you drag the list.
  3. Merge and download. Run the merge, then download the combined PDF. Open it and scroll through every section before you share it.
  4. Optional cleanup. If the result includes blank or duplicate pages, use the delete-pages or split tools on the same site, still in your browser.

Passwords and permissions

A password-protected PDF may need to be unlocked first, and only if you are allowed to do that. Do not bypass protection on a file you do not have the right to change.

Why local merging matters

Identity documents, contracts, and payslips do not belong on a random upload form. A browser-based merge keeps a copy on your device unless you later choose to send the file yourself.
